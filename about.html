About Us

What is this Portal?

The MMUST Student Complaint Portal is an online platform designed to make it easier for students to communicate their complaints and concerns to university administration.

Our Purpose

To provide students with a convenient and accessible way to report issues affecting their university experience.

Our Objectives

  1. Simplify the complaint submission process.
  2. Improve communication between students and administration.
  3. Reduce unnecessary physical visits to university offices.
  4. Improve complaint documentation.
  5. Encourage timely feedback through email.

Our Commitment

We aim to make complaint reporting easier, more convenient and accessible.

Thank you for using the MMUST Student Complaint Portal.

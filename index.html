Welcome to the MMUST Student Complaint Portal. This platform allows students to report their concerns and communicate with university administration.

Why Use This Portal?

  1. Report complaints conveniently from anywhere.
  2. Communicate directly with university administration.
  3. Receive feedback through your registered email.

How Does It Work?

  1. Fill in the complaint submission form.
  2. Submit your complaint to the administration.
  3. Wait for feedback through your email.
